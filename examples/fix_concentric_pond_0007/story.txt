雨滴落进静静的池塘。一圈波纹荡开，外面又是一圈一样的波纹，再外面还是一圈——每一圈都是里面那一圈放大后的样子，一圈套着一圈，直到池边。

A single raindrop falls on a still pond. One ring spreads out, then an identical ring outside it, then another — each ring the same shape as the one inside, only larger, nested outward to the shore.
