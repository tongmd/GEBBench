Each ripple ring is the previous one under a **scaling map**, so the pattern is a fixed point of scale-similarity.

$$R_{n+1} = \lambda \cdot R_n, \qquad \lambda > 1,$$

and the whole family is invariant under the same map $S_\lambda$ that carries any ring to the next:

$$S_\lambda(\{R_n\}) = \{R_n\}.$$

Concentric self-similar rings are the visual signature of $f(x)=x$ under scaling — the same form recurring at every radius.
