A serene traditional Chinese ink-wash and light-color landscape painting. A misty mountain; on it a small temple; inside, through the open door, an old monk sits telling a story to a young monk. On the wall behind them hangs a painting — and that painting shows the SAME mountain, the SAME temple, the SAME two monks, smaller; and inside THAT scene, another painting of the same scene, smaller again — a picture within a picture within a picture, receding infinitely toward the center (mise en abyme, Droste effect). Exactly three clearly visible nested levels before it fades into mist. Calm, contemplative, muted ink tones with soft gold light. No text, no words, no letters anywhere.
